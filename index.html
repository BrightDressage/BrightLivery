Before your horse arrivesPassport, vaccinations and health checks

To protect every horse on the yard, we ask for the following.

All stays

  • Valid passport and registered microchip
  • Flu and tetanus vaccinations up to date
  • Third-party liability insurance for your horse
  • Next of kin details, in case of an accident
  • Signed livery agreement

Full and Holiday Livery

  • Clear strangles blood test, taken within two weeks of arrival
  • Recent worm egg count
  • Vet, farrier and insurance details
