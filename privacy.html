Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it and what your rights are.

Who we are

This website is run by Bright Livery Services, Cork Farm, Long Hill, Old Wives Lees, Canterbury, Kent, CT4 8BN. We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

For any questions about this policy or your information, email hello@brightlivery.co.uk or call 07771 365693.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and anything you tell us about you and your horse.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: your contact details and the content of your messages.
  • Clients: your contact and next of kin details, your horse's details, passport, vaccination and health records, vet, farrier and insurance details, your livery agreement, and billing records.
  • Website use: if you accept analytics cookies, information about how you use the site, such as pages viewed, device and approximate location.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for automated decisions about you.

Who we share it with

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and receives enquiry forms
  • Google, for analytics if you accept cookies, and for the fonts on this site
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message us there
  • Our email provider and our accountant
  • Vets, farriers and other professionals, and insurers, where needed to care for your horse or handle a claim

We will share information with anyone else only if the law requires it, or in an emergency involving a person or horse.

Some of these providers store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the UK-US data bridge or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a client relationship: up to 12 months
  • Client records: while you are a client, then up to 6 years, in case of a legal claim
  • Invoices and payment records: 6 years, as required by HMRC
  • Google Analytics data: up to 14 months

Keeping it safe

We use reputable providers and limit access to your information to those who need it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it
  • ask for your information in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email hello@brightlivery.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, contact us first at hello@brightlivery.co.uk and we will acknowledge your complaint and look into it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
